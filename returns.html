Return & Refund Policy

Clear guidelines for returns, replacements, cancellations and refunds.

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Introduction

Rajput Traders supplies fresh bakery products to shops, hotels, caterers and event customers. Many of our products are perishable, so return and refund requests are checked carefully, one by one. We always aim for a fair and clear solution.

Eligible Return and Replacement Cases

You may ask for a return or replacement if:

Wrong Product Delivered

Damaged Product Received

Expired Product Delivered

Missing Products in Delivery

Manufacturing Defect

Verified Quality Issue

Non-Returnable Products

Returns are generally not accepted for:

  • Opened or used products.
  • Products damaged after delivery.
  • Fresh bakery products stored improperly after delivery.
  • Change of mind after order confirmation.
  • Customized or specially arranged bulk orders.

Replacement Policy

Replacement is preferred whenever possible. It depends on product availability, and an approved replacement is delivered as early as reasonably possible.

Refund Policy

Refunds are made only after a request has been reviewed and approved. The refund method (for example, the original payment method or bank transfer) and the time it will take are confirmed with you when your request is approved.

Order Cancellation

You can ask to cancel an order before it is confirmed or dispatched. Once an order is dispatched, cancellation may not be possible.

Bulk and Event Orders

Bulk and event orders need 25% advance payment, or confirmation on WhatsApp. Once packing or procurement has started, the advance may become partly or fully non-refundable, depending on how far the order has progressed.

Your Responsibilities

To help us solve the problem quickly, please:

  • Check products as soon as they are delivered.
  • Report any issue the same day, on WhatsApp or phone.
  • Send photographs if we ask for them.
  • Keep the product until we have checked it.

Quick Answers

Can I return opened bakery products?

No. Opened products are not eligible for return.

How long does a refund take?

The time depends on the approved refund method and is confirmed when your request is approved.

Can I cancel a bulk order?

Before confirmation or dispatch, yes. Once packing or procurement has started, the advance may not be fully refundable.
