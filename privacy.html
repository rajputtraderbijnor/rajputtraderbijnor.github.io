Privacy Policy

How Rajput Traders handles information you send us through this website.

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

Introduction

This page explains how Rajput Traders handles information when you use this website. We are a wholesale bakery distributor in Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh, and we keep things simple: this website has no accounts, no online payments and no shopping cart.

Information We Receive

We only receive information that you choose to send us, for example through the Contact form, WhatsApp, a phone call or email. This may include:

Contact Details

Your name, mobile number and email address.

Business Details

Your shop or business name, where you share it.

Order Details

Products, quantities and wholesale needs you tell us about.

Delivery Details

Address, town and preferred delivery time for your order.

Messages

Any note or question you send us.

How the Contact Form Works

When you press send on our Contact form, the website prepares a WhatsApp message with your details and opens WhatsApp on your phone. The message is sent only when you send it from WhatsApp. This website does not store your form details on any server of its own.

How We Use Information

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you wholesale prices.
  • To confirm, pack and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about an order or delivery.
  • To keep our own business records, such as orders and invoices.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only when needed to deliver your order (for example, with a delivery person), when the law requires it, or with your permission.

Third-Party Services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. To show fonts and icons, pages load files from Google Fonts and jsDelivr. These services may receive technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads those files, under their own privacy policies. WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms. If you choose Hindi in Settings, the page is translated by Google Translate, which is loaded from Google only at that time and works under Google’s own privacy policy. Product names and business details stay in English. We do not run advertising trackers on this website.

Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. Your choices in Settings (theme, text size, language), your enquiry list and, if you allow it, your name and address for order forms are saved only on your own phone or computer and are never sent to us until you send a WhatsApp message. You can clear them any time from Settings. Third-party services we load, such as Google Translate when you choose Hindi, may use their own cookies or technical storage.

Your Choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details you have sent us by calling or messaging us. We will do this unless we must keep a record for tax or legal reasons.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please get in touch.
